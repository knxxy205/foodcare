# System Flow

1. Admin creates and configures a donation program.
2. Donatur registers publicly, selects a program, and submits a donation.
3. The public site records the payment as successful.
4. The program total is updated and dana terkumpul is recorded.
5. Admin purchases food and records the purchase.
6. Purchased food is received into the warehouse and stock is updated.
7. Relawan sorts incoming food stock.
8. Relawan packages sorted food into distribution-ready units.
9. Admin runs warehouse capacity simulation.
10. Route generation is allowed only when the latest simulation status is `Optimal`.
11. Admin generates distribution routes.
12. Relawan executes distribution to beneficiaries.
13. Relawan uploads proof of delivery.
14. Reports summarize donations, inventory, distributions, and volunteer activity.
